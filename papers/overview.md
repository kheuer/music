## A CRITICAL SURVEY OF RESEARCH IN MUSIC GENRE RECOGNITION
- Surveying papers from 2013-2022 (560 publications)
#### Categories:
- main functions: a review of research, a contribution to evaluation methodology, or an experimental work
- for experimental work: For each experimental work we note the data, experimental approach, and figure of merit it applies.
- We also note the extents to which any publication engages with work critical of MGR as a research problem, as well as genre theory
#### Research questions:
- Is the experimental design Classify and figure of merit accuracy still the most frequent, despite their noted serious flaws threatening the validity of conclusions drawnfrom them.
- Is GTZAN still the most used public dataset, despite its noted faults?
- How have these faults been considered or even reconsidered in the past decade?
- How has all this new research in MGR engaged with work that is critical of MGR as a research problem?
- How has all this work engaged with genre theory such as?
#### Dimensions
- Publication type, Dataset, Experimental Design, Figure of Merit, Referencing, Motivation for MGR
